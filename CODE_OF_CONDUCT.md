# Code of conduct

Faiden welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information.
- Respect consent, project scope, and maintainers' time.

These rules apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Contact the maintainer through the contact options on [@simonfunk's profile](https://github.com/simonfunk) for sensitive conduct reports. Avoid disclosing private details in public issues. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly; no specific response-time guarantee is offered.
